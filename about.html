About SKMEI

Guangdong SKMEI Watch Manufacturing Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise located in Zhaoqing National High-tech Industrial Development Zone, China. With 13,000 square meters of modern factory space and more than 300 professional employees, SKMEI is one of China's leading watch manufacturers.

"SKMEI" was officially registered in China in 2009 and expanded its trademark protection to more than 200 countries worldwide in 2014.

SKMEI provides a complete range of watches including digital watches, children's watches, quartz watches, mechanical watches, smart watches and Azan watches. We also provide professional OEM and ODM services for global partners.

"Quality First" is our company principle. SKMEI is certified with ISO9001, BSCI, CE and RoHS and cooperates with international brands worldwide.

SKMEI continues to focus on quality, innovation and customer satisfaction, aiming to become a leading global watch manufacturer.
